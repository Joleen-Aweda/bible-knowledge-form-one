Preface

This text book, Bible Knowledge for Secondary Schools Student’s Book has been written specifically for Form One students in the United Republic of Tanzania. It has been written in accordance with the 2023 syllabus for Ordinary Secondary Education. The book consists of four chapters, namely: The concept of the Bible and Bible knowledge, God’s creation, the fall of man and the formation of the Patriarchs in the Bible. Each chapter contains activities and exercises. You are encouraged to do all the activities and exercises, as well as other assignments that your teacher will provide. However, for excellent performance and for the acquisition of competencies expected from the study of Bible Knowledge, you are required to read the Bible thoroughly according to the syllabus.

Additional learning resources are available in the TIE e-Library at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
