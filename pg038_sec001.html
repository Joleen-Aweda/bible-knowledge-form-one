The descendants of Cain

Cain went and dwelt in the land of Nod. There Cain knew his wife and got a son whom he named Enoch. Cain built a city in the name of his son Enoch. The descendants of Cain were as follows: Enoch, Irad, Mehujael, Methuselah and Lamech.

Lamech married two wives namely Adah and Zillah. Adah had sons namely: Jabal - His descendants dwelt in tents and had cattle, and Jubal - His descendants played lyre and pipe. Zillah had a son whom she named Tubal-Cain. He made all kinds of bronze and iron tools and had a daughter whom she named Naamah.

The concept of polygamy in the Bible

Polygamy is a marital state in which a person is married to more than one wives as opposed to the marriage between one man and one woman, known as monogamy. Polygamy exists in different forms. When a man is married to many spouses is called polygyny while when a woman is married to more than one husband is called polyandry. According to the Bible, Lamech was the first man to practice polygamy. He contradicted God’s principle of monogamy. Based on the creation story, God instituted marriage between one man and one woman (monogamy). In the New Testament, Jesus explicitly states that a man should have only one wife (Mathew 19:3-9). The Apostle Paul writing at different times insisted that, church leaders must at all times, uphold the practice of monogamy (1 Timothy 3:2).
