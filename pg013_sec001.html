The style of writing in the Bible

The Bible was written in different styles such as narratives, poems, sayings, stories, letters, proverbs, songs, prayers, sermons and prophecies.

The purpose of writing the Bible

The Bible was written for different purposes such as warning, teachings, corrections, and insights (2Timony 3:16).

Importance of studying Bible Knowledge

We get so many benefits from studying Bible Knowledge. The following are some of them: The study of Bible knowledge helps the learner to develop critical thinking and improve communication skills. Again, it helps the learner to get the correct understanding of the scriptures. Moreover, it helps us to get knowledge from the Bible and put it into practice in life. Furthermore, the study of Bible knowledge aids us to know our divine origin and destination, and to get insight on how to acquire eternal life. Still the study of Bible knowledge helps us to know God. “And this is eternal life, that they know you the only True God and Jesus Christ whom you sent” (John 17:3). It also supports us to have good relationships with others, to be servant leaders in society and avoid corruption (Exodus 18:21; 1 Samuel 12:3; Hosea 9:9) eventually to be responsible citizens. Last but not the least, the study of Bible knowledge prepares the learner for further biblical studies.
