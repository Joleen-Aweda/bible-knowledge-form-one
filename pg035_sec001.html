The concept of sin

Sin in Hebrew is ‘khata’ means fall or miss a target. According to the Bible sin is an intentional transgression against God’s command or order. It can be a thought, word or deed that destroy our relationship with God and neighbour.

Sin as violation of God’s rule and moral order, results from the fall of man, lack of knowledge of God, deficiency of trust in God’s providence and disobedience to God. Furthermore, sin originates from dissatisfaction, temptation by the devil, selfishness and human wicked ambitions.

Implications of sin

Sin has many consequences including alienation of human beings from God i.e. deprivation of God’ grace. It also brings broken relationship between God and creation. Furthermore, sin brings pain and suffering to human beings. Consequently, it causes physical and spiritual death. Besides, sin increases enmity, exploitation, and inequality. In addition, sin brings curse upon the land and it is a source of family conflicts and breaking of marriage.
