Three steps of unifying man and woman in marriage

A man leaves his father and mother, the man clings to his wife, the two of them become one body through love and commitment. Figure 2.4 shows a man and a woman.

Marriage according to the Book of Genesis

Basically, marriage is a permanent union between man and woman not otherwise. Biblically speaking, sodomy-and presumably same-sex unions must be condemned because they violate a natural order of heterosexual monogamy ordained in the Bible and across our African cultures. Key characteristics of marriage in Christian tradition are: Mutual love, permanent commitment, procreation, and unconditional respect.
