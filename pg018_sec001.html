The two main parts of the Book of Genesis

The book of Genesis has two main parts.The first part covers Genesis 1-11.This part presents the creation and expansion of the human race.The second part is the story of the Patriarchs, the ancestors of Israel.This covers Genesis 11:10-50:26.
