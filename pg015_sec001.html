Pentateuch

The first five books of the Pentateuch (Genesis to Deuteronomy) are foundational to all Scripture and rank one of the most important sections of the Bible. As knowledge of the Gospels is essential for understanding the New Testament, equally is the Pentateuch crucial to the Old Testament, and for that matter to the whole Bible.
