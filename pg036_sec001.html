By examining the lives of Cain and Abel we will gain valuable insight into our own spiritual journey. We will discover how to develop desire for worship, resistance to temptation and walk in righteousness.

Cain and Abel were involved in murder. This happened when Cain and Abel brought their offerings to the Lord. Cain brought an offering of fruit of the ground, and Abel brought to the Lord an offering of firstling of his flock and their fat portions. Abel was a generous shepherd, he offered with love, devotion and faith. Conversely, Cain was a miserly farmer. He brought simple fruit of the ground. The Lord accepted Abel’s offerings, and rejected Cain’s offerings. The Lord understood the intentions of their hearts. Cain was angry and his countenance fell. He became jealous of Abel. And the Lord asked Cain, why are you angry, and why has your countenance fallen? If you do well, will you not be accepted? and if you do not do well, sin is couching at the door; its desire is for you, but you must master it (Genesis 4:6-7).
