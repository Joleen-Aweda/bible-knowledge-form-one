Main events in the Pentateuch

There are four main events in the Pentateuch. These are: The creation, the formation of biblical Patriarchs, the giving of the Covenant at Sinai, the liberation and guidance from captivity to the Promise Land.

The Concept of the Book of Genesis

The Book of Genesis is the first book in the Bible. It is divided into two main parts, with chapters 1-11 telling the story of God and the whole world, and chapters 12-50 focusing on the story of God, a man and his family.

The word Genesis is a Greek word, which means ‘Origin’ or ‘Birth’ or ‘Beginning’. Genesis or Bereshit in Hebrew, explains the origin of the universe, human race, as well as the origin of sin, suffering and death. It also describes the origin of the Jewish people, and how God intervened to repair the damage caused by sin.
