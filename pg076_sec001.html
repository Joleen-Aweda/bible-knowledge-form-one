Reuben made an agreement with Jacob to slay his two sons if he would not bring Benjamin but Jacob refused to let Benjamin to go with them.

The homage which his brothers paid to Joseph was the fulfillment of his first dream. His brothers knelt at his feet, just as he had seen them in the dream, suppressing his deep emotions. Joseph pretended not to know his brothers because he wanted to find out whether they had repented of the evils they had committed against him.

Joseph’s brothers return to Egypt

When famine became severe, and there was no grain at home, Jacob said, “Go again, buy us a little food” (Genesis 43:2). Judah reminded him of Joseph’s words unless they go with Benjamin they should not see his face. Jacob (Israel) said why did you treat me so ill as to tell the man you had another brother? (Genesis 43:6). Judah insisted their father to let them take Benjamin along with them otherwise they would not live but die. At last, Jacob agreed and gave them the following present to Joseph: A little balm, a little honey, Gum, and myrrh, pistachio nuts, almonds, double amount of the previous money and some fruits. And Benjamin was allowed to go with them under agreed conditions.

Joseph’s final test to his brothers

Joseph received his embattled brothers for the second time and commanded the steward saying, “Fill the men’s sack with food as much as they can carry and put each man’s money in the mouth of his sack” (Genesis 44:1). He even ordered the steward to put his silver cup in the sack of the youngest. After they had left, Joseph commanded the steward to follow after the men, and when you catch them, ask them why they had stolen my silver cup. When he overtook them, the cup was found in Benjamin’s sack.

Therefore, they rent their clothes and every man loaded his donkey and they returned to the city, and they all bowed to Joseph and asked him to allow them to be his slaves. Joseph refused and he wanted them to go, except Benjamin who was found with the cup.

Judah requested Joseph to allow them to go with Benjamin otherwise their father would die of sadness. “For how can I go back to my father if the lad is not with me? I fear to see the evil that would come upon my father” (Genesis 44:34). Joseph decided to put his brothers to another final test to find out their attitude towards their brother Benjamin, whether they cared about him or not.
