Rebekah said to Isaac, “I am weary of my life because of the Hittite women (wives of Esau). If Jacob marries one of the Hittite women of the land what good will my life be to me?” (Genesis 27:46).

God’s providence and protection to Jacob

Isaac called Jacob, blessed him, and told him that he shall not marry one of the Canaanite women. He told him to arise and go to Paddan-aram, to the house of Bethuel, to his mother’s father and take a wife from one of the daughters of Laban, his mother’s brother. God would bless and make him fruitful and multiply. When Esau saw that his wives did not please his parents, he went to Ishmael and took Mahalath the daughter of Ishmael as his wife. Jacob left Beer-sheba and went toward Haran. He reached at a certain place and stayed there because it was night and the sun had set. He took one of the stones, put under his head as a pillow, and slept. He dreamed that there was a ladder reaching to heaven and behold the angels of God were descending and ascending, and the Lord stood above it and said, “I am the Lord, the God of Abraham your father and the God of Isaac” (Genesis 28:13).
