Chapter Two

God’s Creation

Introduction

God created the world. In this chapter, you will learn about creation, you will also clarify man’s responsibility for God’s creation, and you will learn the importance of taking care of life and the environment. The competencies developed will enable you to appreciate God’s creation, to maintain procreation, and to sustain God’s creations. You will also learn to do things systematically, and, furthermore, you will be able to take care of God’s creations including the environment.

Meaning of God’s creation

Creation is an act of bringing something into existence. God’s creation is the making of the earth, the heavens and everything in it. God created the whole universe without using pre-existing materials (ex- nihilo) (Genesis 1).

Creation accounts

Creation accounts are the biblical stories of creation found in Genesis 1 -2. In the Bible, there are two creation accounts.

The first creation account

The first creation story in the book of Genesis begins with the opening words, “In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth. The earth was without form and void, and darkness was upon the face of the deep, and the Spirit of God was moving over the face of the waters” (Genesis 1:1-2).
