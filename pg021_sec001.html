The second creation account

The second creation account pays attention to the creation of human beings and their environment. God created man and woman using existing materials. The man created by God was called Adam and the woman created by God was named Eve. The name Adam, Adamah in Hebrew, means ‘of the ground’ or ‘from the earth.’

Differences between first and second creation accounts

Table 2.1:

Comparison between the two creation accounts

First Creation Account

Second Creation Account
It gives a general explanation of all creation. It gives a specific explanation of the creation of man and woman.
God created the universe from nothing (ex- nihilo). It is practical oriented.
It gives a record of what God created on each specific day. It does not give account of creation based on days.
It is a long creation account. It is a short creation account.
God created by the use of His word. God created by action.
