Chapter One

The concept of the Bible and Bible Knowledge

Introduction

The Bible is the word of God to humankind.

The Word of God has been granted to people in different times and languages.

The Bible has a message for human salvation and sustainability.

Salvation is a gift to man through faith as revealed in the Bible.

God instructs man through the Bible.

Therefore, in this chapter, you will learn about the concept of the Bible and Bible Knowledge, the classification of the books in the Bible, the concept of the book of Genesis, the relationship between Bible Knowledge and other subjects, and the importance of learning Bible Knowledge.

The competencies developed will enable you to explain the meaning of the Bible, Bible Knowledge and its importance, such as to know God’s will, solving daily life challenges, maintaining peace with others, being united with God, building the spirit of faithfulness to God and living responsibly in the society and ultimately having an eternal life.

Meaning of the Bible

The word ‘Bible’ comes from a Greek word biblion, which means a book.

In its plural biblia, which means books.

There are several definitions of the Bible, some of them are highlighted as follows:

The Bible is a collection of Sacred Writings.

It is the Christian scriptures, consisting of the Old and New Testaments.

It is a book like no other.

The Bible is the Word of God, giving the message to humankind concerning the history of our Salvation.

Therefore, the Bible is the word of God through which He is directly speaking to human being and the creation.

The meaning of Bible Knowledge

Bible Knowledge is a subject that deals with the knowledge about God.

It deals with accurate studying, understanding, observing, interpreting and applying biblical facts into daily life.
