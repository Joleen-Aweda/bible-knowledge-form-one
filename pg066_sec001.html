Among the worst consequences of forced marriage is the occurrence of divorce cases which in reality do not conform to God’s plan, and may indeed lead misery among children. The victims in some circumstances may attract criminal charges because of doing criminal offences that could be considered important to rescue him/her from such a forced bond. In actual fact, forced marriages do not guarantee sustainability, since they lack couple’s readiness or consent.
