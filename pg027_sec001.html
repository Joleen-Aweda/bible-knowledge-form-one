Marriage is essentially God’s plan as we read from Genesis 2:18-24. True marriage is between man and woman. God commands man and woman to leave their parents, and become husband and wife. They are united to be one body. Marriage is a holy union. God instituted it in the garden of Eden with our first parents, Adam and Eve. Furthermore, marriage is a permanent union and the married people are expected to stay together until death, in marriage there is no room for divorce.

Commitment:

Is a state of being dedicated to someone (Genesis 4:24, Ecclesiastes 9:9, Mark 10:9, Ephesians 5:22,33). In marriage, both wife and husband are dedicated to each other in mutual love. Their planning and decision-making are done in a commitment manner for the sake of their family. In marriage, commitment means to keep on loving and treating the partner with respect and dignity.

Procreation:

Although the primary purpose of marriage is companionship, procreation comes second and is equally essential for a healthy family. In marriage, companionship and procreation are tightly linked. According to the Bible, marriage is for bearing and raising children. Children are a gift from God. In Africa, bearing children is more valuable in the sense that children are a gift from God, and blessings to the parents and community at large. (Genesisi 1:28).

Permanence:

Marriage is permanent (Matthew 19:6). Marriage is for life. Only death shall dissolve marriage (Romans.7:2). There is no room for divorce.

Practical love:

Marriage requires practical love rather than feelings and passions. Both wife and husband have to show charity to each other.

Living symbol of Christ:

Marriage is a living symbol of unconditional love of Christ and the Church (Ephesians 5:23-32).

Honourable:

Marriage is good and honourable (Hebrew 13:4). Marriage is a bond of trust, faithfulness, and relationship.
