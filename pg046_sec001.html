Importance of unity for sustainable development

The story of the tower of Babel teaches us many things, including the significance of unity with God and other members of the community for promoting sustainable development. The people of Babel failed to achieve their goal because they stood outside God’s circle. Hence to achieve a goal be it social or economic we have to work according to the wishes of God and be in union with Him. This element is essential for development. Union with God accomplishes any task, develops positive attitude towards work, and activates awareness of our accountability and responsibility. Likewise, unity enables people in groups to perform their tasks more effectively and collectively. It also encourages co-operation and positive relationships. Besides, it empowers individuals to gain more knowledge and skills from other people. Figure 3.15 portrays a symbol of unity.

The role of communication in sustainable development

Communication is a-two-way process that involves transferring of information from one person or group to another. It can be face to face, through the use of communication devices or in writing. Nowdays mass media are common means through which information is sent. Mass media are means of mass communication such as broadcasting, publishing and the internet. Through the use of these means information is sent to many people at a time.

For sustainable development communication is very essential, since it caters for information exchange, establishes consensus among people with divergent opinions and interests, and it facilitates the building of technical know-how.
