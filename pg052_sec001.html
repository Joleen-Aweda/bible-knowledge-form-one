Covenant confirmation (Genesis 15: 17-21)

The Lord confirmed His covenant with a visual sign - a smoking firepot and a flaming torch passing between the carcasses.

He affirms His promise to give Abram’s descendants a vast stretch of land, populated by diverse tribes.

The natives in the Promised Land (Canaan)

The Bible considers the Canaanites and groups associated with them, the Amorites, Hittites, Jebusites, Hivites, Perizzites, and Girgashites as the pre-Israelite inhabitants of the land of Canaan.

According to tradition, these early inhabitants of the land are referred to as the seven nations of Canaan.

The Promised Land was put under the Israelites following the conquest led by Joshua and the conquest that was completed during the period of Judges.

Canaan refers to the land that was situated in the territory of the southern Levant.

In today’s understanding, Canaan encompasses the land of Israel, the West Bank and Gaza, Jordan, and the southern parts of Syria and Lebanon.
